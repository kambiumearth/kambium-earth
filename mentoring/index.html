Nature-Connected Depth Mentoring

A 1:1 mentoring journey on the land for nature-based educators, facilitators and mentors who are already running sessions.

Portugal · Sintra & nearby · or remote

Book a discovery call → How it begins

Out on a limb with your groups?

This is for you if you're already doing the work: educators, forest school leaders, facilitators and guides running sessions now.

Mentoring supports your students to grow. Your growth works the same way.

Drawn to this work and not yet holding groups? Start with our Deep Nature Connection Mentoring Foundation Training, 11 to 14 March 2027.

What changes

You leave with a practice that is yours, and a clearer sense of who you are as a mentor.

  • Review and feedback on your programs.
  • Sharper eyes for reading your students and your group.
  • Steadier footing on the difficult edges: hazards, safety, group dynamics.
  • Someone who knows your work well enough to mirror it back.

Four Threads

  • Hands-on skill development

    Develop your hard skills: friction fire, cordage, shelter and craft, plus activities you can bring to your own participants. Practised with feedback.

  • Nature-connection mentoring

    Core routines, natural flow learning, the art of questioning and story, applied to your groups. We can rebuild a real program of yours around them.

  • Naturalist knowledge of place

    Birds, plants, tracks, weather, water and the land you work on.

  • Personal nature connection

    Your own relationship with the living world, and whatever is coming up at your own edge.

We may also work on reading your students, group dynamics, hazards and safety, and live practice.

What's Included

Mentoring sessions
90 minutes, about twice a month or more often. In person in Sintra and nearby, or remote.
Weekly field debrief
After a program you run, send me a voice note debriefing it. I reply within a day and support you to learn and adapt.
Quick questions
Dealing with inclement weather? Comms with parents? Fire kit got soaked? I'm here to think along with you. Send it by text or email and I reply within a day.
Practice commitment
Set by you at the close of each session. Specific and achievable, reviewed when we next meet.

Async support is a companion to the sessions. If a question needs real depth, we bring it to the next session.

90 minutes. Your work fills it. Here's the structure:

How It Begins

  1. First

    A discovery call

    Thirty minutes to talk about your work, what you want from mentoring, and whether we're a fit. We set the length of your journey and the price together here.

  2. Then

    Choose your journey

    Start with 4 or more sessions, about twice a month or more often.

    Set the first session

Joshua Glass

I mentor for deep nature connection with adults and educators, and also with families and youth. Through ancestral skills and wilderness survival practices like fire, shelter, foraging and tracking, I help people learn a place well enough to love and care for it. I mentor youth and guide rites of passage with Twin Eagles Wilderness School and have apprenticed with Maine Primitive, studying the 8-Shields Cultural Mentoring model. I studied social design and community building at Zuyd University in NL and in 2024, co-founded Kambium•Earth-Based Learning in Portugal. I have taught at iArts, part of Zuyd University of Applied Sciences in Maastricht, NL. I return yearly to Massachusetts, where I grew up, to meet with mentors and family.

Approach and lineage

Rooted in deep nature connection mentoring: core routines, natural flow learning, the 8 Shields medicine wheel and the art of questioning. These are practices I've learned from mentors with many years in wilderness education and depth mentoring, several of whom learned directly from people with intact place-based ancestry and cultural practices.

Practicalities

Where
In person around Colares and Sintra, Portugal, often in the Sintra-Cascais Natural Park, with an exact meeting point agreed upon before the session. This can also be your own program venue. Remote from anywhere.
When
Days and times are agreed with you before the first session.
Weather
We work outside in most weather, so come dressed for it. Each session has a fair-weather site and a sheltered one. Storms, lightning or an IPMA red warning mean we move, go remote or reschedule at no cost.
What to bring
Layers, waterproofs and boots; water; a notebook and pen; a knife (fixed blade is best); and anything live from your own work.

Questions

Is this therapy?

No. This is mentoring and coaching for your practice as an educator and facilitator, and it doesn't replace therapy, counselling, clinical supervision or medical advice. The decisions you make with your groups remain yours.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. Anything about your practice, your groups or your struggles stays between us.

Can I do it all remotely?

Yes. In-person sessions are around Colares and Sintra, and remote works anywhere.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need, so we set the price together on the free discovery call. Book a call to start.
