What We Do

Nature Connection
Depth Mentoring

Group and 1-on-1 mentoring for youth, families, and adults to build awareness, resilience, and belonging through direct relationship with the Earth—rooted in the 8 Shields cultural mentoring model.

Ancestral Skills &
Wilderness Survival

Learn fire-making, shelter-building, foraging, tracking, and other practical earth-based skills that support self-reliance and reconnection with natural systems. Nature-connected survival skills help us depend less on destructive systems and more on the living Earth—and our relationship with it.

Nature-Connected
Educator Training

Training and consulting for educators, mentors and facilitators to design and lead nature-based learning. Gain practical frameworks for guiding children & youth through cooperation with natural systems—bridging hands-on earth skills, mentoring methods, and experiential learning for thriving learning communities.

Who We Are

JOSHUA EMERSON GLASS

Joshua Glass is a nature connection mentor working with families, youth, and adults. Through ancestral skills and wilderness survival practices like fire, shelter, foraging, and tracking, he helps people learn a place well enough to love and care for it. He mentors youth and guides rites of passage with Twin Eagles Wilderness School and has apprenticed with Maine Primitive (Maine), studying the 8-Shields Cultural Mentoring model. He studied social design and community building at Zuyd University in the Netherlands and in 2024, co-founded Kambium•Earth-Based Learning in Portugal. He returns yearly to Massachusetts.

MELINA FEUVRIER-ETEVENARD

Practitioner and teacher of permaculture and regenerative farming, alongside crafting, cooking, and the art of wilderness awareness. She's cultivated her skills in communities and regenerative farms throughout Europe. Driven by a passion for nurturing both ecosystems and cultures, Melina has worked with youth and adults in pedagogical farms and nature camps. A guardian of cycles, she guides herself and others in aligning human lifestyle with the rhythms of the living world. She transforms curiosity into unforgettable learning experiences, and uses wild-foraged edible plants to create vitality-rich, nutritionally balanced and delicious meals. Her love for nature, crafting, and togetherness shapes her approach as a chef and nature connection guide.

Our Lineage

Jeannine Tidwell is the Co-Founder and Co-Director of Twin Eagles Wilderness School, located in Sandpoint, Idaho. She and her husband founded the school around 2005 after moving to the area, focusing on reconnecting people with nature, community, and themselves through wilderness skills and experiential education. The school emphasizes kinship with place and the broader bio-region, forming deep relationships to foster a sense of belonging and connection. Jeannine draws upon decades of nature mentoring and personal growth experience, inspired by a mentor who helped guide her toward her best self. She has been actively mentoring people of all ages for over 20 years and leads programs focused on mentoring, leadership, personal growth, and rites of passage for girls and women. The school and Jeannine's work stress earth-based living skills and building community through transformative outdoor experiences.

Tim Corcoran is the founder of Purpose Mountain and co-founder of Twin Eagles Wilderness School. He's a certified Nature-Based Purpose Guide dedicated to personal mentoring and supporting people's deep connection with wild nature to discover their purpose and live their vision. He focuses on deep nature connection mentoring, cultural restoration, and rites of passage. With over two decades of experience, Tim leads men’s groups, vision quests, and transformational ceremonies rooted in teachings from his mentors and indigenous elders. He is dedicated to healing cultural divides, fostering holistic health, and nurturing an earth caretaker movement, living as a heart-centered father and mentor.
