Kambium for Boys · Winter 2027

A mentoring group for boys, 9 to 14

We're shaping a multi-week program for boys on the land around Sintra and Colares: fire, shelter, tracking, tools, story and circle, with time for each voice to be heard. Before we set dates, place and price, we want to hear from you.

Who:
boys aged 9 to 14
Where:
Sintra-Colares area, exact place to be confirmed
When:
between January and May 2027, rhythm shaped by your answers
With:
Joshua Glass and Dylan Vincent

Questions? Write to info@kambium.earth
